# Title Compression Rules

Long paper decks need strong title hierarchy. Main titles should guide scanning; subtitles carry explanation.

## Length Rules

- Main title: 6-14 Chinese characters by default.
- Main title above 18 Chinese characters must be split into short title + subtitle.
- Chapter title: 8-16 Chinese characters, can be larger and more atmospheric.
- Card title: 2-8 Chinese characters, preferably noun phrase.
- Bottom takeaway: one complete sentence, concise and specific.

## Title Roles

- Main title states the concept or claim.
- Subtitle explains the full question or nuance.
- Body paragraph carries source logic.
- Cards carry structured paper objects.

Do not let a main title do paragraph work.

## Rewrite Pattern

Long title:

`现实悖论：绿色合规为什么既像成本，也可能是效率来源？`

Better:

- Main title: `绿色合规的双重属性`
- Subtitle: `为什么它既带来成本压力，也可能成为效率来源`

Long title:

`非线性检验：ER² 用来识别成本挤出与创新补偿的转换`

Better:

- Main title: `非线性转换`
- Subtitle: `ER² 用来观察成本挤出是否转向创新补偿`

## QA

Before export:

- Scan all main titles longer than 18 Chinese characters.
- Split long titles into short title + subtitle.
- Make card titles short and parallel.
- Avoid pure section labels unless subtitle states the question.
