# Navigation System

Top navigation is a chapter positioning system, not tiny page-header text. The default style is refined deep teal: a stable left research-title mark plus visually full active/inactive chapter chips, with the active chapter anchored by a yellow chip, deep-teal treatment, underline, or short color block.

## Default Rules

- Keep top navigation on ordinary content pages.
- Navigation area should occupy about 7%-10% of slide height when fuller labels need room.
- Use 思源宋体 CN Heavy or the same Source Han Serif family for every navigation label. Do not mix navigation labels across sans/serif/decorative fonts.
- Left short title: about 16-20 pt, stable across content pages, and aligned with the header grid.
- Active chapter chip: about 12-16 pt. Prefer 14-16 pt when there are 4-5 short labels; use 12-14 pt when there are 6+ labels or long section names.
- Inactive chapter chip: about 10-14 pt, usually 2 pt smaller than active on the same slide. Preserve chip/label shape but reduce fill contrast, font weight, opacity, or color strength.
- Active section: deep teal/yellow chip, bold, visually anchored by underline, short color block, or soft highlight.
- Inactive sections: deep teal or dark gray/black-gray, smaller than the active treatment but still full and readable.
- To keep labels full, widen nav items, slightly raise the header area, or compress chapter names before shrinking active below 12 pt or inactive below 10 pt.
- Navigation and title area must have stable spacing.
- Navigation should align with the visual grid and not look pasted on.
- Active highlight blocks, short underlines, and navigation color chips may use light shadows, low-opacity gradients, or soft strokes. These effects should make the navigation more polished without pressing into the title or body content.

## Visual Components

Allowed:

- left title marker integrated with header line;
- deep-teal header strip or subtle pale green header band;
- thin divider line;
- short active underline with soft shadow or low-opacity gradient;
- active yellow or deep-teal color block/chip with restrained gradient fill;
- inactive low-contrast chip/label with deep teal, dark gray, pale teal, or translucent fill;
- chapter progress decoration;
- small page number paired with footer or nav system.

Avoid:

- isolated repeated `▶ ▶` symbols with no design relationship;
- tiny gray navigation that disappears in contact sheet;
- navigation squeezed into a 2%-3% header;
- active chapter labels below 12 pt or inactive labels below 10 pt in the default style unless the user explicitly asks for a very compact header;
- decorative fonts;
- active and inactive labels too similar to read.
- heavy gradients, thick shadows, or bright glows that reduce title/body readability.

## Title Relationship

Main title should sit near the navigation but not fight it. If the title is long, compress it and move the explanation into subtitle.

## QA

Check the contact sheet:

- Active section is immediately identifiable.
- All nav labels use 思源宋体 CN Heavy or the same Source Han Serif family.
- Left short title is about 16-20 pt, active chapter chips are about 12-16 pt, and inactive chips are about 10-14 pt when there is enough width.
- Inactive chips are normally about 2 pt smaller than active chips and visibly lighter, but still readable.
- Inactive sections remain readable and visually balanced.
- Navigation height and spacing are consistent.
- Active underline/block has restrained shadow or gradient polish when used.
- No nav label overlaps the title, line, page number, or content.
