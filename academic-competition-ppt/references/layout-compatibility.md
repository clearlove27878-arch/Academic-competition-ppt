# Layout Compatibility

Use this when long-form content meets PPT layout limits.

## Priority

Academic expression wins over visual minimalism. If content is important and crowded, split the slide before shrinking text or deleting logic.

If content is sparse, do the opposite: add original-text substance, explanation panels, a paper object, background chrome, and a bottom takeaway before adding more pages.

## Dense Content Rules

- More text: split into explanation page + evidence page.
- Complex concept: split definition, measurement, and model role.
- Large table: crop, split into full-context and close-up, or convert key rows into result cards while preserving source context.
- Complex formula: use formula crop plus editable explanation box.
- Many variables: group by dependent/core/mediator/moderator/control instead of one huge table.
- Many robustness checks: one doubt per page or grouped by doubt type.
- Sparse content: add a source-driven paragraph, side explanation, keyword tags, bottom takeaway, or visual object rather than leaving large white areas.

## Visual Safety

- Preserve aspect ratio for charts, tables, formulas, and screenshots.
- Do not stretch text-bearing images.
- Avoid text overlap with title, navigation, badges, or cards.
- Keep formulas and tables readable in contact sheets and individual previews.
- Do not hide evidence behind decorative backgrounds.
- Ordinary content pages should retain visible navigation, subtle background decoration, and bottom takeaway chrome.

## Template Compatibility

When a visual template is active:

- Use the template for color, navigation, title treatment, and rhythm.
- Keep paper evidence and explanation layout readable.
- Override the template if it forces tiny tables, decorative thumbnails, or empty poster-style pages.
