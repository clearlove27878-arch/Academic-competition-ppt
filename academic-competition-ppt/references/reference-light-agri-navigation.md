## Visual System

- **Background:** content pages use white or very pale gray geometric backgrounds. Cover and closing pages may use full-bleed agricultural photos with a deep green translucent horizontal band.
- **Primary colors:** deep green around `#206040`, `#306040`, and `#407060`.
- **Secondary colors:** sage green, mint green, pale gray, and soft green-tinted shadows.
- **Accent color:** controlled bright yellow/gold around `#FFC000`, mainly for key words, numbers, arrows, and emphasis marks.
- **Typography:** page titles are heavy, deep green, and large. Body text is mostly black; key terms may be highlighted in yellow. Cover titles may use a calligraphic or heavy Song-style Chinese font when available.
- **Shape language:** rounded green title pills, light cards, thin dividers, process arrows, native tables, and photo/evidence containers. Avoid over-decoration and avoid turning every page into the same card grid.

## Refined Top Navigation

- Place the navigation at the top of body pages, with the page/chapter title on the left and section labels arranged horizontally on the right.
- Typical labels can follow the reference rhythm: `项目背景 / 技术创新 / 商业模式 / 团队维度 / 财务分析 / 应用推广 / 战略规划`, adapted to the actual paper or project.
- The active section must be visibly larger, bold, and deep green, with a short deep green underline beneath it.
- Inactive sections should be smaller black or dark gray text; keep them readable but visually secondary.
- Do not place a bottom rectangular block behind the navigation. Use only a thin divider line and/or the active-section underline.
- A lightweight top-left double-triangle/arrow identity mark may be used, but it must not overpower the slide title.
