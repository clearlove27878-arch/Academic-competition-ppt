# Source Selection Rules

Use this when deciding what source text enters a PPT. The deck should be faithful to the paper without copying low-value prose.

## Source Classes

### Original Anchors

Keep or closely paraphrase:

- Research question and core puzzle.
- Core definitions and variable definitions.
- Model equations and identification strategy.
- Data source, sample, measurement logic.
- Main findings or expected tests.
- Contribution statements.
- Policy/method implications and limitations.

Treatment: preserve the idea and technical meaning. Use short quotes only when exact wording matters; otherwise use faithful Chinese paraphrase.

### Technical Original Text

Includes formulas, models, identification strategy, coefficient definitions, robustness design, mechanism design, and variable construction.

Treatment: convert into three layers:

`original meaning -> plain-language explanation -> economic intuition`.

Do not show technical text alone. Pair it with why it is needed and what mistake it avoids.

### Background Original Text

Includes policy context, market facts, real-world contradictions, literature motivation, and field background.

Treatment: turn into reality paradox, literature gap, story opening, timeline, or problem-chain page. Keep cautious wording when facts are external or time-sensitive.

### Low-Value Original Text

Usually exclude:

- Generic literature-list paragraphs with no gap.
- Repeated sample details already shown in tables.
- Boilerplate policy slogans.
- Repeated definitions after the concept has been explained.
- Decorative or rhetorical sentences that do not support a claim.

Treatment: omit by default, or compress into a note if it is needed for completeness.

## Fidelity Rules

- Do not invent results, coefficients, samples, policy dates, or mechanisms.
- Do not over-polish wording until the original claim changes meaning.
- If a source is a research plan, preserve uncertainty: `拟检验`, `预期方向`, `待估计`, `若结果显示`.
- Each core chapter should contain at least one source anchor, table, formula, figure, or faithful paraphrase tied to the original paper.
- Each major chapter should derive 40%-60% of body content from original anchors, original viewpoints, or faithful paraphrases.
- Each ordinary page should include at least one original thought object: paraphrased claim, definition, measurement rule, model setting, mechanism judgment, result interpretation, or research boundary.
- Default conversion path: `original viewpoint -> faithful paraphrase -> presentation explanation -> page takeaway`.
- AI-generated text should explain, bridge, and adapt for speech; it must not replace the source paper's main substance.
