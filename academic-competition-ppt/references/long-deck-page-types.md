# Long Deck Page Types

Use this as the micro page type library for default long-form paper presentations.

## Micro Page Types

- Reality paradox page: real-world tension or counterintuitive puzzle.
- Problem transition page: what has been shown and what must be asked next.
- Literature gap page: streams of literature and the precise missing piece.
- Concept definition page: one key construct, definition, measurement, and model role.
- Variable measurement page: variable source, formula, unit, expected role.
- Mechanism path page: one causal pathway at a time.
- Formula breakdown page: symbols, coefficient, economic behavior, boundary.
- Coefficient interpretation page: direction, size, significance/expected direction, meaning.
- Identification threat page: reverse causality, omitted variables, selection, measurement error, or timing.
- Result explanation page: one table/figure/panel and its answer.
- Mechanism test page: pathway test and what it adds.
- Heterogeneity story page: subgroup logic and economic reason.
- Robustness rebuttal page: one doubt and one response.
- Research contribution page: theoretical, empirical/method, and policy/method contribution.
- Policy implication page: cautious implication tied to evidence.
- Method insight page: what the design teaches beyond this topic.
- Research boundary page: what the paper cannot prove.
- Q&A page: likely questions and evidence-backed answers.

## Rhythm Rule

A long deck cannot repeat one card-grid layout across the whole presentation. Every 6-8 pages, change rhythm through:

- chapter divider;
- logic-chain return;
- stage recap;
- evidence close-up;
- formula/model walkthrough;
- table/figure dominant page;
- comparison or contradiction page;
- synthesis page.

## Page Count Guidance

- 28-45 slides: research plan or ordinary paper explanation.
- 40-60 slides: empirical paper deep explanation.
- 50-70 slides: complex formulas, methods, literature seminar, or dense evidence battery.

Short 15-20 slide structures are explicit summary variants only.
