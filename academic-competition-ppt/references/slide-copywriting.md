# Slide Copywriting

Default copy style is report-script-compressed prose: clearer and more connected than bullet fragments, shorter and more slide-friendly than the original paper. Long-form paper decks should read like a condensed presentation script, not a pile of short cards.

## Page Copy Components

Each ordinary content page may include:

- Main title: compressed, usually 6-14 Chinese characters.
- Subtitle: the page question or explanatory nuance.
- Explanation: 1-2 source-driven short paragraphs with causal connectors, about 50-120 Chinese characters each.
- Keywords: 2-4 terms, variables, methods, or results.
- Paper object: formula, model, table, figure, variable card, hypothesis, source anchor, or diagram.
- Bottom takeaway: one sentence saying what this page proves, clarifies, limits, or opens next.

## Writing Rules

- Prefer sentences that explain causality: because, therefore, if, only when, by contrast, this means.
- Do not use pure noun headings such as `基准回归` unless the subtitle states the question.
- Do not paste long source paragraphs.
- Do not default every page to bullets. Use paragraphs when explaining a source idea or mechanism.
- Do not let generic labels such as `经济含义` or `解释边界` become the main content.
- Do not reduce complex concepts to one-line slogans.
- Keep original meaning, direction, uncertainty, and conditions.
- In empirical pages, say what question the test answers.
- In research-plan pages, use `拟检验`, `预期`, `待估计`, `若研究发现` where appropriate.

## Density

Higher text density is allowed in long paper reports, but the text must have structure:

- 1 main claim.
- 1 paper object.
- 1-2 source-driven explanation paragraphs.
- 1 bottom takeaway or transition.
- enough layout density that the page does not feel empty.

If a page needs more than two explanation paragraphs, split it.
